Good to know

  • Hours and days off can change. Check the listing on the map before you go.
  • The legal drinking age in Japan is 20. Some places may ask for ID.
  • Several bars here take cash only: iBrew, Craft Beer Scissors and Beer Otaku Spice (PayPay is the only cashless option there). Carry some yen.
  • Izakaya often add a small seating charge. Beer Otaku Spice does in the evening. Fujita Saketen has none.
  • Bar Malt Road is closed on Sundays, public holidays and the third Monday. Fujita Saketen is open on weekdays only, from 17:00 to 21:00.
