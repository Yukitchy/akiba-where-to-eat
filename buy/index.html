Good to know

  • Hours, closed days and payment come from each shop's official page where one exists. Items marked "not confirmed" were not on any official page, so check before you go.
  • Shops may close on public holidays or change hours without notice. Check the listing on the map before you go.
  • Many shops sell used items with a condition grade. Ask the staff if you are unsure.
  • Bring your passport if you want to shop tax-free. Only Animate says so on its official page.
  • Walking times are straight-line estimates from the station and are approximate.
